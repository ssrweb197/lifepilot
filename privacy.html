Privacy Policy

LifePilot · Last updated: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how LifePilot handles information when you use the LifePilot website and app.

Information we handle

  • Account information: your email address and authentication details are handled by Supabase Authentication when you create an account or sign in. LifePilot does not receive or store your plain-text password.
  • App information: tasks, habits, check-ins, goals, routines and focus-minute totals that you enter.
  • Local storage: when you use LifePilot without signing in, app information is saved in your browser's local storage on that device.
  • Cloud storage: when you sign in, LifePilot uses Supabase to load and sync your app information to your account.

How information is used

Information is used to provide account access, save and restore your planner data, synchronise your data across supported devices, maintain app functionality and respond to support requests.

Service providers

LifePilot is hosted through Cloudflare Pages. Account authentication and signed-in app-data storage are provided through Supabase. The Supabase JavaScript client is loaded from jsDelivr. These providers process information as needed to deliver their services and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share account or planner information with advertisers. Information may be processed by the service providers described above to operate LifePilot, or disclosed where required by law or to protect the service and its users.

Security and your choices

We use Supabase authentication and user-scoped database access policies for signed-in data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your password private and use a secure device. You can use the app without signing in, but cloud synchronisation requires an account.

Retention and deletion requests

Local information remains in your browser until you clear the site's data or remove it. Cloud information may remain in your account until deleted. To request help with access, correction or deletion of account-related information, email brandblitzmotivation@gmail.com from your registered email address. We may need to verify your request before acting.

Children

LifePilot is a general productivity tool and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children without appropriate consent.

Changes

We may update this policy as LifePilot changes. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

Contact

Privacy questions: brandblitzmotivation@gmail.com.
