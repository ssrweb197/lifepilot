Frequently Asked Questions

LifePilot · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Is LifePilot free to use?

The current LifePilot version is available to use without a subscription. If pricing or paid features are introduced, the details will be shown clearly before purchase.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use the app in your browser without signing in. Your data will remain in that browser on that device. Sign in to enable cloud synchronisation.

How do I create an account?

Enter your email address and a password in the LifePilot account area, then choose Create account. If email confirmation is enabled, open the confirmation message and follow its link before signing in.

How does cloud sync work?

After you sign in, LifePilot loads your account's saved planner data and syncs changes to your Supabase account. Use the same account on another supported device to access your synced data.

Will signing out delete my data?

Signing out does not intentionally delete your browser's local data or your cloud account data. For privacy, sign out on shared devices.

What if I forget my password?

Contact brandblitzmotivation@gmail.com for assistance. Do not send your existing password.

Can I use LifePilot on a phone?

Yes. LifePilot is designed to work in modern mobile and desktop browsers.

Can LifePilot provide medical or financial advice?

No. LifePilot is a productivity tool, not a professional advice service. See the Disclaimer.

How do I report a problem?

Email brandblitzmotivation@gmail.com with a short description, your browser/device details and any error message. Never include your password or verification code.
