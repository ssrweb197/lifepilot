About LifePilot

LifePilot · Last updated: November 10, 2026

LifePilot is a simple productivity and personal-planning app designed to help people organise everyday tasks, build consistent habits, track meaningful goals and create routines.

Our purpose

We believe progress is built through small, practical steps. LifePilot brings daily planning, habit check-ins, goal progress, routines, a focus timer and decision prompts together in one easy-to-use workspace.

What you can do

  • Plan and track daily tasks.
  • Build habits and record check-ins.
  • Set goals and monitor progress.
  • Create morning, afternoon and evening routines.
  • Use a focus timer and simple decision prompts.
  • Sign in to sync app data with your LifePilot account.

Our approach

LifePilot is intended to be straightforward, accessible and useful on desktop and mobile devices. We aim to improve the experience while treating user information responsibly.

Small steps make remarkable progress. Make today count.
