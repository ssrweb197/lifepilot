Help & Support

LifePilot · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Need a hand using LifePilot? Start with the steps below. If you still need help, email us.

Sign-up or sign-in trouble

  • Check that your email address is entered correctly.
  • Use a password with at least six characters.
  • If email confirmation is enabled, confirm your email before signing in.
  • Try refreshing the page and signing in again.

Cloud sync trouble

  • Check your internet connection.
  • Make sure you are signed in to the correct LifePilot account.
  • Wait briefly and refresh the page.
  • If an error remains, send us the exact error text (but never your password or secret keys).

Using LifePilot

Use Daily Planner for tasks, Habits for check-ins, Goals for progress tracking, Routines for daily steps, and Focus & Decisions for the timer and decision prompts.
